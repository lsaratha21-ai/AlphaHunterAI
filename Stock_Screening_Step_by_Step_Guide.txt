================================================================================
                    STOCK SCREENING STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
              Using Screener.in, Moneycontrol, and Chartink
                          JULY 2026
================================================================================

This guide provides exact steps to find stocks using your screening criteria
on the three major Indian stock screening platforms.

TARGET: Find stocks that pass ALL 5 avoid criteria and 8/8 consider criteria
- Large-cap: Market cap > ₹20,000 Cr
- Mid-cap: Market cap ₹5,000 - ₹20,000 Cr  
- Small-cap: Market cap < ₹5,000 Cr


================================================================================
                        PLATFORM 1: SCREENER.IN
================================================================================

URL: https://screener.in

STEP 1: Go to Screener
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open browser and go to: https://screener.in
2. Click on "Screener" in the top navigation
3. You'll see the screener interface with filters

STEP 2: Set Market Cap Filter (Do this 3 times for each category)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FOR LARGE-CAP:
- Filter: Market Cap
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 20000
- Click "Add Filter"

FOR MID-CAP:
- Filter: Market Cap
- Operator: Between
- Value: 5000 and 20000
- Click "Add Filter"

FOR SMALL-CAP:
- Filter: Market Cap
- Operator: Less than
- Value: 5000
- Click "Add Filter"

STEP 3: Add Financial Filters (Apply to all categories)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Filter 1: Sales Growth
- Filter: Sales growth (CAGR)
- Period: 3 years
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 30
- Click "Add Filter"

Filter 2: Profit Growth
- Filter: Profit growth (CAGR)
- Period: 3 years
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 40
- Click "Add Filter"

Filter 3: Debt to Equity
- Filter: Debt to equity
- Operator: Less than
- Value: 0.5
- Click "Add Filter"

Filter 4: Interest Coverage
- Filter: Interest coverage
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 5
- Click "Add Filter"

Filter 5: Current Ratio
- Filter: Current ratio
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 1.5
- Click "Add Filter"

Filter 6: ROE
- Filter: ROE
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 15
- Click "Add Filter"

STEP 4: Add Ownership Filter
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Filter 7: Promoter Holding
- Filter: Promoter holding
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 50
- Click "Add Filter"

STEP 5: Run the Screener
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Click "Export to Excel" button (top right)
- Save the file as: "Screener_LargeCap_[Date].xlsx"
- Repeat for Mid-cap and Small-cap

STEP 6: Manual Verification (For each stock from results)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each stock in the Excel file, manually check:

1. AVOID CRITERIA (Must PASS all 5):
   [ ] Not doubled in 3-6 months without catalyst
   [ ] Profits NOT declining (check last 4 quarters)
   [ ] Debt NOT very high (already filtered)
   [ ] Liquidity NOT poor (already filtered)
   [ ] NOT social media hype without fundamentals

2. CONSIDER CRITERIA (Need 8/8):
   [ ] Profit growth ≥ 40% (already filtered)
   [ ] Sales growth ≥ 30% (already filtered)
   [ ] Trading sideways 3-6 months (check chart)
   [ ] Breakout on 3X volume (check chart)
   [ ] Low debt (already filtered)
   [ ] Promoter holdings ≥ 50% (already filtered)
   [ ] Emerging sector (check sector classification)
   [ ] Management confidence (check investor presentations)


================================================================================
                        PLATFORM 2: MONEYCONTROL
================================================================================

URL: https://www.moneycontrol.com/stocks/marketstats/ind-screener.php

STEP 1: Go to Moneycontrol Screener
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open browser and go to: https://www.moneycontrol.com/stocks/marketstats/ind-screener.php
2. You'll see the stock screener page

STEP 2: Set Market Cap Filter
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FOR LARGE-CAP:
- Market Cap: Large Cap (already preset)
- Or select Custom: > ₹20,000 Cr

FOR MID-CAP:
- Market Cap: Mid Cap (already preset)
- Or select Custom: ₹5,000 - ₹20,000 Cr

FOR SMALL-CAP:
- Market Cap: Small Cap (already preset)
- Or select Custom: < ₹5,000 Cr

STEP 3: Add Financial Filters
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Filter 1: Net Profit Growth
- Select: Net Profit Growth (YoY %)
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 40
- Click "Add"

Filter 2: Sales Growth
- Select: Sales Growth (YoY %)
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 30
- Click "Add"

Filter 3: Debt-Equity Ratio
- Select: Debt-Equity Ratio
- Operator: Less than
- Value: 0.5
- Click "Add"

Filter 4: P/E Ratio
- Select: P/E Ratio (TTM)
- Operator: Less than
- Value: 30
- Click "Add"

Filter 5: Current Ratio
- Select: Current Ratio
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 1.5
- Click "Add"

Filter 6: ROE
- Select: ROE (%)
- Operator: Greater than
- Value: 15
- Click "Add"

STEP 4: Run the Screener
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Click "Search" or "Apply Filters" button
- Results will appear below
- Click "Export to Excel" if available
- Or manually copy results to Excel

STEP 5: Manual Verification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same as Screener.in - manually check avoid and consider criteria
Use your MASTER framework for detailed evaluation


================================================================================
                        PLATFORM 3: CHARTINK
================================================================================

URL: https://chartink.com/screener

STEP 1: Go to Chartink Screener
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open browser and go to: https://chartink.com/screener
2. Click on "Create New Scan" or use existing scans

STEP 2: Use Pre-built Scan Formula
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Copy and paste this scan formula:

```
{
  "exchange": "NSE",
  "segment": "EQ",
  "filter": [
    {
      "left": "NET PROFIT",
      "operation": "GREATER_THAN",
      "right": "40",
      "period": "365"
    },
    {
      "left": "SALES",
      "operation": "GREATER_THAN",
      "right": "30",
      "period": "365"
    },
    {
      "left": "NET DEBT",
      "operation": "LESS_THAN",
      "right": "0.5",
      "period": "0"
    },
    {
      "left": "PROMOTER HOLDING",
      "operation": "GREATER_THAN",
      "right": "50",
      "period": "0"
    },
    {
      "left": "MARKET CAP",
      "operation": "LESS_THAN",
      "right": "5000",
      "period": "0"
    },
    {
      "left": "CURRENT RATIO",
      "operation": "GREATER_THAN",
      "right": "1.5",
      "period": "0"
    }
  ]
}
```

STEP 3: Modify for Different Market Caps
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FOR LARGE-CAP:
Change market cap filter to:
```
{
  "left": "MARKET CAP",
  "operation": "GREATER_THAN",
  "right": "20000",
  "period": "0"
}
```

FOR MID-CAP:
Change market cap filter to:
```
{
  "left": "MARKET CAP",
  "operation": "BETWEEN",
  "right": "5000,20000",
  "period": "0"
}
```

FOR SMALL-CAP:
Keep as is (less than 5000)

STEP 4: Run the Scan
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Click "Scan" button
- Results will appear in table format
- Export to Excel using "Export" button

STEP 5: Manual Verification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same as other platforms - manually check avoid and consider criteria

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Remove duplicates
3. Create unique stock list
4. Organize by market cap

DAY 3-5: Manual Verification (2-3 hours)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each stock:
1. Check 5 avoid criteria (quick pass/fail)
2. If pass, check 8 consider criteria
3. Use MASTER framework for detailed evaluation
4. Score each stock (target 8/8)

DAY 6: Final Selection (30 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Review all scored stocks
2. Identify 8/8 pass stocks
3. Identify 7/8 pass stocks
4. Create final watchlist
5. Prioritize by score
