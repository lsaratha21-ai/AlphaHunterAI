================================================================================
                        AVOID CRITERIA (RED FLAGS)
================================================================================

These are the 5 criteria that MUST ALL PASS before considering any stock.
If ANY of these FAIL, immediately REJECT the stock.

1. STOCKS THAT HAVE ALREADY DOUBLED WITHOUT NEW CATALYST
   - Price increased 100%+ in last 3-6 months
   - No fundamental business changes to justify the move
   - Driven by speculation, hype, or market momentum
   - RISK: Overvalued, likely to correct sharply
   
   HOW TO IDENTIFY:
   - Check 3-month and 6-month price performance
   - Look for news/events during the period (earnings, contracts, sector tailwinds)
   - If no clear catalyst, AVOID

2. DECLINING PROFITS
   - Net profit decreasing for 2+ consecutive quarters
   - Profit margins shrinking over time
   - Earnings growth negative YoY or QoQ
   - RISK: Business deterioration, competitive pressure
   
   HOW TO IDENTIFY:
   - Review last 4-8 quarters of P&L statements
   - Check profit margin trends (gross margin, operating margin, net margin)
   - Compare with industry peers

3. VERY HIGH DEBT
   - Debt-to-Equity ratio > 2.0
   - Interest coverage ratio < 2.0 (EBIT/Interest)
   - Debt increasing faster than assets/business growth
   - RISK: Financial distress, bankruptcy risk in downturns
   
   HOW TO IDENTIFY:
   - Check Debt-Equity ratio on balance sheet
   - Calculate Interest Coverage Ratio = EBIT / Interest Expense
   - Review debt trends over last 3-5 years

4. POOR LIQUIDITY
   - Current ratio < 1.0 (Current Assets / Current Liabilities)
   - Quick ratio < 0.5 (Quick Assets / Current Liabilities)
   - Cash conversion cycle > 90 days
   - RISK: Unable to meet short-term obligations
   
   HOW TO IDENTIFY:
   - Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities
   - Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities
   - Check cash flow statement for operating cash flow trends

5. SOCIAL MEDIA PROMOTION WITHOUT BUSINESS IMPROVEMENT
   - Heavy promotion on Twitter, YouTube, Telegram without fundamentals
   - Influencer-driven price pumps
   - No corresponding improvement in financials or operations
   - RISK: Pump and dump schemes, manipulation
   
   HOW TO IDENTIFY:
   - Cross-reference social media buzz with actual business news
   - Check if company has released meaningful updates
   - Look for unusual volume spikes without news


================================================================================
                        CONSIDER CRITERIA (GREEN FLAGS)
================================================================================

These are the 8 positive criteria. A stock needs to meet at least 4 of these
to PASS the screening. The more criteria met, the better the stock.

1. PROFIT GROWTH ≥ 40%
   - Net profit growth ≥ 40% YoY or QoQ
   - Consistent profit growth over multiple periods
   - Improving profit margins
   - INDICATOR: Strong business momentum, operational efficiency
   
   HOW TO VERIFY:
   - Compare latest quarter profit with same quarter last year (YoY)
   - Compare with previous quarter (QoQ, seasonally adjusted)
   - Check profit margin expansion

2. SALES GROWTH ≥ 30%
   - Revenue growth ≥ 30% YoY or QoQ
   - Sustainable sales growth (not one-time contracts)
   - Market share gains or industry growth
   - INDICATOR: Business expansion, demand strength
   
   HOW TO VERIFY:
   - Compare latest quarter revenue with same quarter last year
   - Analyze revenue composition (recurring vs one-time)
   - Compare with industry growth rates

3. TRADING SIDEWAYS FOR 3-6 MONTHS
   - Price consolidation in narrow range (±10-15%)
   - Building base for potential breakout
   - Low volatility during consolidation
   - INDICATOR: Accumulation phase, low risk entry point
   
   HOW TO VERIFY:
   - Check 3-month and 6-month price charts
   - Calculate price range: (High - Low) / Average Price
   - Look for consolidation patterns (rectangle, triangle)

4. BREAKOUT ON 3X+ VOLUME
   - Price breaks out of consolidation range
   - Volume 3x+ higher than average daily volume
   - Strong momentum with institutional participation
   - INDICATOR: Institutional buying, trend start
   
   HOW TO VERIFY:
   - Calculate average daily volume over 20-50 days
   - Check if breakout day volume ≥ 3x average
   - Confirm price breaks above resistance level

5. LOW DEBT
   - Debt-to-Equity ratio < 0.5
   - Interest coverage ratio > 5.0
   - Net-debt free or minimal debt
   - INDICATOR: Financial flexibility, lower risk
   
   HOW TO VERIFY:
   - Debt-Equity ratio should be < 0.5
   - Interest Coverage = EBIT / Interest Expense > 5.0
   - Check if company has net cash (Cash > Debt)

6. PROMOTER HOLDINGS ≥ 50%
   - Promoter holding ≥ 50% (shows confidence)
   - Promoter holding stable or increasing (not selling)
   - No pledged shares (or minimal pledge)
   - INDICATOR: Management confidence, alignment with shareholders
   
   HOW TO VERIFY:
   - Check shareholding pattern on exchange filings
   - Look at promoter holding trends over last 1-2 years
   - Check for pledged shares percentage

7. EMERGING SECTOR
   - Company operates in high-growth emerging sector
   - Sector expected to grow 20%+ annually over next 3-5 years
   - Government policy support or structural tailwinds
   - INDICATOR: Favorable macro environment, sector momentum
   
   HOW TO VERIFY:
   - Research sector growth projections (industry reports, government data)
   - Check for policy support (PLI schemes, regulatory changes, incentives)
   - Analyze secular trends (digitalization, green energy, EV, manufacturing, etc.)
   - Compare sector growth vs overall GDP growth

8. MANAGEMENT CONFIDENCE & GUIDANCE
   - Management provides clear, realistic guidance
   - Confident about future growth prospects
   - Good track record of meeting guidance
   - Transparent communication with shareholders
   - INDICATOR: Leadership quality, execution capability
   
   HOW TO VERIFY:
   - Read recent investor presentations and conference call transcripts
   - Check if management has met previous guidance
   - Look for consistency in communication
   - Assess management's track record and credibility
   - Check for insider buying (shows confidence)

   - Profit growth: > 40% (CAGR 3 years)

   - Promoter holding: > 50%

   URL: https://www.moneycontrol.com/stocks/marketstats/ind-screener.php

   - Market Cap: Small-cap

   - Sales Growth (YoY): > 30%
   - Debt-Equity Ratio: < 0.5
   - P/E Ratio: < 30
   - Current Ratio: > 1.5
   - ROE: > 15%

   URL: https://chartink.com/screener
   Use scan formula for:
   - Net profit growth > 40%
   - Sales growth > 30%
   - Debt-Equity < 0.5
   - Promoter holding > 50%
   - Market cap < ₹5,000 Cr

4. TRENDLYNE
   URL: https://trendlyne.com
   Use Screener with:
   - Sales growth (3-year CAGR): > 30%
   - Profit growth (3-year CAGR): > 40%
   - Debt-Equity: < 0.5
   - Promoter holding: > 50%
   - Market cap: Small-cap
