COMMON TECHNICAL PATTERNS:
1. Fresh 52-week or all-time highs
2. Trading above all key moving averages
3. Bullish MACD on weekly/monthly charts
4. Expanding Bollinger Bands (volatility with upward bias)
5. Strong volume confirmation on breakouts
6. Systematic accumulation (up day volume > down day volume)

ENTRY STRATEGY:
- Wait for breakout confirmation with volume
- Enter on pullbacks to key moving averages (20/50-DMA)
- Use stop-loss below recent swing lows
- Position size: 2-3% per stock (small caps are volatile)

TARGETS:
- First target: 10-15% from entry
- Second target: 25-30% from entry
- Trail stop-loss once first target achieved

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Strict stop-loss at 5-8% below entry
- Avoid averaging on losing positions
- Book partial profits at first target
- Monitor RSI for overbought conditions (above 70)
